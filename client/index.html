Search Help

To search, all you need is to enter the city, but to be sure you have the correct country, you can type in the city followed by a comma and the country, as an abbreviation or not. If the city has a shorthand, like Saint Paul -> St. Paul, proper punctuation is necessary.

Examples: Dublin, IE; Dublin, Ireland; Dublin, US; Dublin, OH, USA; St. Paul

*The comma is obligatory!
